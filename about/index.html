About

Owner-driven private hire from mid-Cornwall

PHD Taxi Services is Roger's business. He owns it, answers the phone and drives you himself - a Professional Hired Driver based near Bodmin and Luxulyan, not a call centre or a rank of strangers.

Meet Roger

The owner is the driver

Roger owns and runs PHD Taxi Services, and he is the name you will see in its TripAdvisor reviews. When you book, it is Roger who confirms your quote and Roger who drives you - there is no dispatcher and no handover to someone you have never met.

He runs pre-booked private hire from a base near Bodmin and Luxulyan - local airport and station transfers, long-distance journeys across the South West and driven day tours around Cornwall.

Every route that's regularly booked carries a published guide price, confirmed exactly before you travel. There's no meter and no night-time surcharge - local knowledge meets competitive prices.
