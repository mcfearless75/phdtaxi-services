Booking policies

How pricing and cancellations work

No night surcharge

The quote you get for 2pm is the same one you get at 2am. Most firms charge more between 23:00 and 07:00 - PHD doesn't.

10% off return bookings

Book your return leg at the same time and it's 10% off that fare.

Secure contactless payment

Pay by card on the day, no cash required.

Students and Blue Light welcome

Concessions available for students and emergency services personnel - mention it when you book.

48-hour cancellation policy

All prepaid bookings must be cancelled within 48 hours of travel. Refunds outside that window are discretionary.
